## Slide 2 — The Problem

**64,700 tons of trash. Every day.**

- Municipal collection misses the pile behind the sari-sari store, the vacant-lot dumping, the post-fiesta debris
- **100,000+ informal waste workers** already recover up to 30% of urban waste — invisible, unrated, often earning under $1/day
- Barangays owe segregation, collection & MRFs under **RA 9003** — still the law's weakest links, 20+ years in

**The gap isn't equipment or willingness. It's trust.**

> *Three failing actors: the resident who'd pay, the worker who'd collect, the barangay that's accountable. Nothing connects them.*

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

**A marketplace with a profession inside.**

Post the mess → a verified, rated **Green Collector** accepts → tracked live to your gate → cleans up → proves it with a photo → paid via GCash/Maya/cash, confirmed by both sides.

**The professionalized collector is the product. The app is the coordination layer.**

> *Emphasize: not a gig app. Every job builds the collector's credential — that's the point of the whole system.*

## Slide 5 — The Credential (Our Moat)

**A career ladder, not piece-rate gigs.**

Verified → Top-Rated → **Certified (TESDA)** → Team Lead → Barangay Coordinator

- Derived from verified in-app work history — can't be faked, can't be ported
- Top-Rated = 20+ paid pickups at ≥4.8 rating, computed live from job data
- Upper rungs = the roadmap this funding builds

> *"A FoodPanda rider's rating dies with the app. A Green Collector's credential is a profession — and it only grows here."*

## Slide 6 — Market

**42,046 barangays. 27M households.**

- 10% of households × one ₱150 pickup/month ≈ **₱4.7B/yr** marketplace volume → **~₱560M/yr** at a 12% take
- LGU dashboard across barangays: **~₱1.26B/yr** B2G ceiling
- Why now: GCash **94M** users, Maya **50M+**; digital payments = 57% of retail volume; gig apps normalized rated, tracked service work

> *Present as honest estimates: "these are cited public figures with stated assumptions, and the pilot exists to validate them."*

---

## Slide 7 — Business Model

**Two lines, one flywheel.**

1. **12% take rate** per pickup once in-app rails (PayMongo/GCash) launch — ₱18 on a ₱150 bounty, earned via instant payout + payment guarantee
2. **Barangay/LGU SaaS** ₱1,500–5,000/mo — waste-hotspot maps, certified-collector roster, RA 9003 reports

Pickups generate the data → the data sells the dashboard → the dashboard legitimizes collectors → more pickups.

> *Preempt the extraction question: the 12% funds verification, instant payout, PPE, and the certification ladder — and collectors net ~₱660/day at 5 pickups.*
